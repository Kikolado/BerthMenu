# BerthMenu privacy policy

BerthMenu doesn't collect personal data, and doesn't send any to its author.

**What stays on your PC.** Your settings, pinned apps, folders, files and
websites, widget settings and notes, and BerthMenu's own icon cache are saved on
your PC only (in BerthMenu's app data folder). Nothing is uploaded.
Settings → Startup → **Export…** saves a copy wherever you choose.

**When BerthMenu goes online.** Only for things you use:

- **Updates** come through the Microsoft Store; BerthMenu itself doesn't check
  for them.
- **Website tiles:** when you pin a website, BerthMenu downloads that site's icon
  from the site itself.
- **Web search** and links (like Report a problem) open in your web browser,
  which then follows its own privacy policy.

**Reading what's on your PC.** To do its job, BerthMenu reads the list of
installed apps, recently used apps and files (Windows' own lists), what's playing
in media apps, the volume of your speakers and apps, and network speed. It uses
these only to show them to you, on your PC.

**Report a problem** opens a GitHub issue in your browser with your BerthMenu
and Windows versions and recent error messages filled in (with your Windows user
name taken out). Nothing is sent until you submit it yourself.

If a later version ever needs to send anything (to check a license, for
example), it will say so, and this page will be updated first.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/Kikolado/BerthMenu/issues).
